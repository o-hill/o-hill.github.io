Highlights

  1. Led software development on ARGOS (Automated RecoGnition Of Species) at Michigan Aerospace Corporation for three years: a microservice stack on Python, Vue.js, MongoDB, Kafka, Docker, Nginx, and GraphQL.
  2. Built the ARGOS species-detection models: CNNs for drone imagery, plus grid prediction and MCMC approaches for mapping density.
  3. Software Engineering Intern on Azure Networking at Microsoft in Redmond (Summer 2018), working on network debugging tooling and latency-sensitive streaming over IGMP.
  4. Researched ecological networks with Dr. Fernanda Valdovinos in my Master's at UMich, using dynamic systems, complex systems theory, and ML on supercomputing clusters.
  5. Built a restaurant wait-time predictor at Arboreal AI with over 80% accuracy, roughly 60% better than human estimates.
  6. Trained a real-time pupil-tracking CNN at 45 fps to detect nystagmus, with a doctor at the University of Michigan hospital.
  7. Built Potatobot, an NLP bot for answering student questions on EECS course forums, for my undergrad senior research project.
  8. Held many roles on the EECS 281 staff at UMich (course admin, guest lecturer, education researcher) for a class of 700–1,000 students per semester.
