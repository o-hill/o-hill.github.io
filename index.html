I build and lead teams that put software into the physical world. As CTO and first employee at TestMachine, I grew an AI security startup to 20 people, built its engineering organization, and won and managed a seven-figure contract with Coinbase. Before that, I set up an eight-person team to deliver Gabon's national timber-traceability system, working directly with government officials and operators across the supply chain. I'm based in Detroit and focused on manufacturing, energy, and building in America.

Projects

  1. TestMachine AI (2022–2026)

    I joined as the first employee and served as CTO, helping raise nearly $7M and growing the company to 20 people. We built AI systems that hunt for security vulnerabilities and then prove they're real by replaying them in a simulated environment, with no AI in the loop. That system, Azimuth, ranked #1 on several public industry benchmarks and found a critical flaw in Ledger hardware wallets, which secure billions in user funds. We disclosed it privately and published it after the fix shipped.

  2. Gabon national timber traceability, Environmental Investigation Agency (2021–2023, advisor since)

    I designed and led a system that tracks every log in Gabon from the forest through sawmills and transport to export, giving businesses and regulators one shared record. I spent a lot of time on the ground with officials and operators. Within two years, 85% of the country's timber volume was recorded digitally, and an anomaly-detection model we built helped cut fraud by an estimated 72%.

  3. Disclose (2026, part-time co-founder)

    An AI treasurer for political committees and non-profits. Much of the work was figuring out what AI can do under campaign-finance law and what it can do reliably with today's models. We took it from customer conversations to pilot customers with $17M in assets committed.

  4. HALAS laser weather monitoring, Michigan Aerospace (2019–2020)

    I built the data pipeline for a laser system that measures weather conditions up through the thermosphere to support DoD, NASA and SpaceX flight testing. HALAS was later acquired by Honeywell.

  5. Drone mapping of invasive species, Michigan Aerospace

    I worked on ML that finds and predicts invasive plant species in aerial drone imagery of wildlife preserves, and led a three-person team that turned it into a tool scientists and land managers could use.
